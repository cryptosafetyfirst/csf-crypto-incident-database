**Coverage Assessment**

Dominant patterns remain covered by existing registry rules.

The principal uncovered pattern concerns token-network and asset-network
compatibility. Multiple residual incidents contain prevention guidance
requiring confirmation that a receiving platform supports the specific
asset on the selected blockchain. This pattern is narrower than the
existing destination-verification rule and was therefore not absorbed
into that rule through reinterpretation.

Post-compromise wallet migration, use of a clean replacement
environment, and forensic-preservation guidance form another residual
group. These mappings remain heterogeneous and do not establish a single
rule boundary.

Wallet-setup and credential-isolation guidance also remains residual
where alignment would require broadening existing rule meaning.

Prevention normalization itself reports continuing mapping friction,
with prevention guidance present but not always conservatively mappable
to the available normalized actions. Governance notes independently
preserve this friction as an advisory signal rather than altering
prevention logic.

The asset-network compatibility residual represents candidate
rule-emergence visibility only. No rule creation determination is made.

Governance escalation threshold status remains undeclared because
explicit governance thresholds were not supplied.

**Governance Determination**

Persistent recurrence is concentrated primarily within existing CSF
rules.

governance-review signal only.

Other residual mappings remain fragmented and advisory.
