**CW08 Prevention Action Normalization Report**

**Stage:** Prevention Action Normalization (Publication)\
**Scope:** CW08 Crypto & Web3 Incident Dataset\
**Total Incidents Analyzed:** 63

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Purpose**

This report measures how explicitly stated prevention guidance from real
incident records maps to a locked, standardized prevention action set.
Guidance is derived directly from the prevention field of each incident
and normalized into a fixed action framework to enable comparable
coverage analysis across weeks.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Methodology Summary**

Prevention actions are mapped exclusively from the **prevention** field
of each incident record.\
Mappings are applied conservatively and deterministically.\
Multiple actions may be mapped to a single incident when explicitly
supported by the text.\
Vague, missing, or unmappable prevention language is not forced into the
action set.\
Incidents with classification warnings are handled conservatively during
mapping.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Data Integrity Notes**

- Total incidents analyzed: **63**

- Incidents with classification warnings: **2** (entries 16, 18)

- Incidents with missing prevention text: **0**

- Incidents with present but unmappable prevention text: **0**

- Entry number integrity issues: **None detected**

Coverage therefore reflects the full dataset and is not reduced by
missing prevention text.

**Key Findings**

The most frequently normalized prevention action is **Verify platform
legitimacy before use (44.4%)**, representing nearly half of all
incidents.

A secondary tier of coverage includes:

- **Avoid unsolicited messages calls and DMs (14.3%)**

- **Maintain complete transaction and tax records (7.9%)**

- **Never share recovery phrases or private keys (7.9%)**

Several prevention actions show zero coverage this week, indicating no
explicit prevention language mapped to those categories in the dataset.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Top Prevention Actions by Coverage**

1.  **Verify platform legitimacy before use (44.4%)**

2.  **Avoid unsolicited messages calls and DMs (14.3%)**

3.  **Maintain complete transaction and tax records (7.9%)**

Combined coverage of top three actions: **66.6% of total incidents**

Coverage is partially affected by classification warnings (2 incidents),
though no prevention text was missing or unmappable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Interpretation (Non-Speculative)**

Prevention guidance this week is concentrated heavily in platform
verification language. A meaningful portion of incidents also explicitly
reference unsolicited contact avoidance and recordkeeping practices.
Several technical and custody-related actions appear absent in explicit
prevention text.

The distribution reflects only what was directly stated in incident
records and does not imply completeness of prevention strategies.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Role in the Weekly Series**

This report serves as a weekly snapshot of normalized prevention
coverage across the CW08 dataset. It enables longitudinal comparison of
prevention action representation across weeks and supports consistent
measurement of guidance patterns over time.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Bottom Line**

Prevention language this week is measurable and concentrated, with
nearly half of incidents explicitly referencing platform verification.
Zero coverage in multiple action categories highlights structural gaps
in explicit prevention framing. The report measures observable coverage
only and does not represent a complete prevention framework.
