**CW04 Prevention Action Normalization Report**

**Stage:** Prevention Action Normalization (Publication)\
**Scope:** CW04 Crypto & Web3 Incident Dataset\
**Total Incidents Analyzed:** **71**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Purpose**

This report summarizes how often specific prevention actions explicitly
appear in recorded crypto and Web3 incident data. Prevention actions are
derived directly from real incidents and mapped conservatively to a
fixed, locked action set without inference or expansion.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Methodology Summary**

Prevention actions are mapped only from the recorded prevention field in
each incident. Mapping is conservative: vague, narrative, or unmappable
text is not forced into actions. Multiple actions may be mapped per
incident, and additional caution is applied when classification warnings
are present.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Data Integrity Notes**

- Total incidents analyzed: **71**

- Incidents with classification warnings: **1**

- Incidents with missing prevention text: **multiple**

- Incidents with present but unmappable prevention text: **present**

As a result, prevention coverage represents a **lower bound** of
explicitly recorded guidance.

**Key Findings**

Explicit prevention guidance most frequently emphasizes platform
verification and skepticism toward unsolicited contact. Other prevention
actions appear sporadically, while several actions do not appear at all
in recorded guidance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Top Prevention Actions by Coverage**

The three most frequently recorded prevention actions are:

- Verify platform legitimacy before use

- Avoid unsolicited messages calls and DMs

- Never share recovery phrases or private keys

Together, these account for **42.2%** of incidents.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Interpretation (Non-Speculative)**

The distribution shows concentration in a small number of explicit
prevention themes, alongside a substantial absence of mappable
prevention language. This reflects reporting limitations rather than
overall preventability.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Role in the Weekly Series**

This report provides a weekly snapshot of explicitly recorded prevention
coverage and supports consistent longitudinal comparison across
datasets.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Bottom Line**

Most incidents contain **no explicitly mappable prevention guidance**.
Where guidance is present, it is concentrated in a narrow subset of
actions, making prevention coverage measurable but incomplete by design.
