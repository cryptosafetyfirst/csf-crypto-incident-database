**governance_notes.md --- CW10**

During the preparation of the CW10 dataset, classification warnings were
observed in several incident records. These warnings indicate instances
where classification boundaries required conservative handling during
incident labeling.

A repeated warning pattern related to subtype classification uncertainty
was also present. This pattern signals potential taxonomy pressure where
certain incidents may sit near category boundaries.

Prevention action normalization proceeded without declared mapping
friction. Prevention guidance contained in the dataset was successfully
interpreted within the existing action set.

Dataset integrity checks confirmed that no reclassification occurred
during weekly preparation, no changes to the incident scope were made,
and no post-generation edits were detected in the finalized artifacts.

These observations are recorded as governance signals for potential
future review.
