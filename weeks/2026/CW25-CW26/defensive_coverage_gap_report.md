dominant prevention failure points observed during the reporting period
are addressed within the current CSF Defensive Canon.

The report is non-public, informational, and non-authoritative. It does
not modify incidents, prevention mappings, governance rules, or

**Coverage Summary**

The dominant prevention failure points identified during the reporting
period correspond to existing educational coverage within the declared
CSF Defensive Canon.

Behavioral failures remain primarily associated with platform
verification, unsolicited communications, exchange fraud, social
engineering, and wallet recovery protection. Operational failures
involving transaction verification, authentication, and approval
management are likewise represented within existing defensive materials.

Governance inputs identify classification ambiguity, taxonomy pressure,
and limited prevention normalization friction; however, these conditions
do not indicate absent educational coverage or previously unsupported
defensive topics.

No dominant prevention failure point was identified that lacks
representation within the currently declared defensive canon.
