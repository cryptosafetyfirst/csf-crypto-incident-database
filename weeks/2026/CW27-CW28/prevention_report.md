**CW27 + CW28 Prevention Action Normalization Report**

**Stage:** Prevention Action Normalization (Publication)

**Scope:** CW27 + CW28 Crypto & Web3 Incident Dataset

**Total Incidents Analyzed:** 63

**Purpose**

This report summarizes prevention guidance derived from real-world
crypto and Web3 incidents. Prevention statements are normalized into a
fixed prevention action set to enable consistent comparison and
longitudinal measurement across weekly datasets.

**Methodology Summary**

Only the prevention field is used for normalization. One incident may
map to multiple prevention actions where explicitly supported. Mapping
is performed conservatively, with reduced confidence where
classification warnings exist. Vague or unmappable prevention text is
not forced into the locked action set.

**Key Findings**

The most frequently normalized prevention action is **Verify platform
legitimacy before use**, followed by **Avoid unsolicited messages calls
and DMs** and **Do not pay fees to unlock withdrawals**. Several actions
were not represented in this dataset, while 11 incidents contained
prevention text that could not be conservatively mapped to the locked
action set.

**Interpretation**

The distribution is concentrated around a small number of normalized
prevention actions, with platform verification representing the largest
share. Classification warnings and unmappable prevention text reduce
confidence in complete coverage but do not alter the reported counts.

**Role in the Weekly Series**

This report provides a weekly snapshot of normalized prevention
coverage, supporting consistent longitudinal comparison of prevention
themes across Crypto & Web3 incident datasets.

**Bottom Line**

Platform verification, avoiding unsolicited contacts, and refusing
withdrawal-fee requests account for most explicitly mappable prevention
guidance in this dataset. The reported coverage is measurable and
comparable across weeks, while remaining conservative where prevention
text could not be reliably normalized.
