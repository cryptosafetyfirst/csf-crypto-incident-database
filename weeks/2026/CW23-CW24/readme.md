**Repository Topology Overview**

The repository is organized into multiple layers:

- Weekly aggregation layer

- Atomic incident layer

- Publication rendering layer

- Governance artifact layer

- Visualization asset layer

Weekly aggregation artifacts provide package-level orientation.

Atomic incident records remain the underlying traceability layer.

Publication artifacts provide human-readable renderings of weekly
outputs.

Governance artifacts provide operational awareness and process
observations.

Visualization assets support publication and communication activities.

**Incident Traceability Reference**

Weekly aggregation artifacts are derived from normalized incident
records maintained within the incident repository structure.

Atomic incident records remain the traceability source for weekly
aggregation artifacts.

Weekly outputs provide orientation to those records without replacing
them.
