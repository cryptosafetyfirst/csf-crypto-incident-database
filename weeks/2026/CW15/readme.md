**Folder Contents Overview**

This weekly folder contains the core artifacts required for
traceability, analysis, and publication:

- **manifest.md** --- the weekly metadata anchor defining scope and
  incident identity

- **incidents.csv** --- the canonical dataset of structured incident
  records

- **taxonomy report** --- structured classification distribution and
  integrity checks

- **prevention report** --- normalized prevention action coverage and
  mapping results

- **governance_notes.md** --- advisory record of observed ambiguity and
  pipeline friction

These artifacts collectively provide a complete, reproducible weekly
snapshot without modifying underlying incident records.
