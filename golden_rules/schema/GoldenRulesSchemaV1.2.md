**The Golden Rules Preventive Intelligence Framework**

**What Are the Golden Rules?**

Golden Rules are immutable, versioned, single-sentence preventive
behaviors derived from recurring crypto and Web3 incident patterns.

They are not advice.

They are distilled behavioral countermeasures extracted from structured
incident intelligence.

Each Golden Rule answers one question only:

**"What single behavior would have prevented this loss?"**

They are:

- Read-only outputs of the intelligence pipeline

- Derived only after incident ingestion is complete

- Version-controlled and governance-bound

- Tested weekly against real incidents

- Focused strictly on behavior, not narrative

They are not explanations.\
They are not opinions.\
They are not detection logic.

They are preventive distillations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**What Golden Rules Are Not**

Golden Rules do not:

- Modify incident records

- Change verdicts, taxonomy, or classification

- Reinterpret intent

- Merge incidents

- Influence future incident ingestion

- Act as automated detection systems

Intelligence informs rules.\
Rules never inform intelligence.

This boundary is absolute.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Why They Exist**

Crypto incidents are complex.

Users have limited attention.

Golden Rules compress recurring failure patterns into strict, measurable
behaviors that reduce loss.

Instead of long post-mortems, users receive:

Clear\
Actionable\
Habit-forming prevention signals

Each rule is:

- Imperative ("Never...", "Always...")

- Specific to a failure mode

- Derived from recurrence density (not severity)

- Time-window anchored

- Explicitly governed

They transform intelligence into defensive clarity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Governance Model**

Golden Rules operate under strict separation from the core intelligence
system.

They must:

- Be derived only after structured ingestion

- Remain failure-mode specific

- Be reproducible from canonical incident data

- Be testable without reinterpretation

- Remain backward compatible unless explicitly deprecated

They evolve only through:

- Version increments

- Explicit governance review

- Formal deprecation

Old versions remain valid for historical analysis.

Rules never silently change meaning.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Weekly Testing Framework**

Every active Golden Rule is tested weekly against the full canonical
incident set for that scope.

For each incident, the rule is evaluated deterministically:

- Prevented

- Not Prevented

- Not Applicable

No narrative reinterpretation.\
No probabilistic scoring.\
No partial credit.

Coverage density is calculated mechanically.

Trend acceleration is monitored.

If recurrence exceeds governance thresholds, a rule may be reviewed ---
but never altered automatically.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Schema Overview**

Each Golden Rule is an immutable, versioned record with explicit fields
including:

Core Identity:

- rule_id

- rule_version

- rule_status

- created_at

- last_reviewed

Logic:

- rule_statement

- applies_to_incident_types

- applies_to_subtypes

- preventive_action_type

- failure_pattern_blocked

Evidence:

- derived_from_window

- first_observed_week

- last_observed_week

- confidence_level

- evidence_incident_count

- evidence_week_count

Governance:

- deprecation_note (if applicable)

Rules are advisory only and never alter underlying incident truth.
